# Known verse differences for manual review

English KJV is the reference for the app's numbering. This list records the
differences identified so far, not a complete verse-by-verse comparison.
Malayalam now uses the Free Bibles India edition, not the original app's
edition. Tamil and Malayalam have not been comprehensively checked against KJV.

## Still different or unavailable in the current Malayalam edition

| KJV reference | What to review |
| --- | --- |
| Genesis 1:5 | The Malayalam source repeats the statement about naming day and night. Both occurrences are retained and flagged. |
| Acts 15:18 | The Malayalam source's wording differs from KJV and does not clearly include KJV's statement that all God's works are known from the beginning. |
| Acts 15:25–26 | The Malayalam source's wording and order cross the KJV verse boundary. Both are shown together at 25; 26 points to 25. |
| Acts 15:28–29 | The Malayalam source combines and reorders these verses. Both are shown at 28; 29 points to 28. |
| Acts 15:34 | No corresponding text was found in the Malayalam source; marked unavailable. |
| Acts 28:29 | No corresponding text was found in the Malayalam source; marked unavailable. |
| Romans 16:24 | No corresponding text was found in the Malayalam source; marked unavailable. |
| Romans 16:25–27 | The Malayalam source combines/reorders the wording. The complete passage is shown at 25; 26 and 27 point to 25. |
| 2 Timothy 3:6–7 | The Malayalam wording crosses the KJV boundary. Both are shown at 6; 7 points to 6. |
| Revelation 13:1 | The source's Revelation 12:18 was moved to the start of 13:1. It says the dragon stood on the sand, unlike KJV, which says “I”. |

## Boundary corrections to verify

These are already mapped to the indicated KJV references; manual review can
check the splits and wording against the surrounding verses.

| KJV reference | Mapping made |
| --- | --- |
| Exodus 7:25 and 8:1 | Split the beginning of source 8:1 into 7:25 and 8:1. |
| Deuteronomy 12:32 and 13:1–18 | Moved source 13:1 to 12:32; shifted chapter 13 and separated/reordered the end of 13:16–18. |
| Deuteronomy 28:68 and 29:1–29 | Moved the end of source 28:68 to 29:1 and shifted chapter 29. |
| 1 Samuel 30:30–31 | Split the source's combined passage. |
| 2 Samuel 17:28–29 | Split the source's combined passage; its word order also differs. |
| Song of Solomon 6:13 and 7:1–13 | Moved source 7:1 to 6:13, shifted the next verses and split source 7:12. |
| 3 John 1:14 | Combined the source's verses 14 and 15, as requested. |

## Differences found in the original app's Malayalam edition

The replacement edition generally has separate entries for these, but their
wording and alignment still merit review:

- Psalm 12:7–8; Nahum 3:8–9; Hebrews 10:24–25: previously combined.
- Psalm 111:9–11 and 1 Timothy 6:21–22: previously split or numbered differently.
- Acts 15:10: the original had an empty duplicate; the replacement has one entry.
- Proverbs 10:0: the original stored a section heading as verse zero; the
  replacement importer omits source headings.

Existing bookmarks keep their saved reference numbers and can point to different
Malayalam wording in remapped chapters.
